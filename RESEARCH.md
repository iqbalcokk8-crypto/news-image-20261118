### 3. BUSINESS — Nvidia posts a record quarter; board will not reinstate Huang
- Nvidia reported third-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue of $115.8 billion, up
  103% from a year earlier and above the ~$109 billion analysts expected.
  Data-centre revenue more than doubled to $107.6 billion (about 93% of
  sales). The company forecast $130 billion for the current quarter.
- On the earnings call, board chairman Harvey Jones said the board had
  unanimously accepted founder Jensen Huang's resignation and "does not

### 4. UNITED STATES — 27 years for the Iran-backed plot to kill Trump
- A US judge in Brooklyn sentenced Asif Merchant, 48, of Pakistan, to 330
  months (27 and a half years) in prison for attempting to commit an act of
  terrorism transcending national boundaries and murder for hire. Merchant is
  a trained operative of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, the US
  Justice Department said; a federal jury convicted him in March 2026.
- Prosecutors said Merchant tried to recruit people in the US in a plan
  targeting President Donald Trump and other prominent politicians — targets

- Sources: Reuters, published 2026-11-18T00:46:15Z

### 5. FINANCE — 15 million files leak at Deutsche Bank
- An international investigation ("Inside Deutsche Bank", more than 200
  journalists from over 50 media organisations, including the Financial
  Times, the New York Times, Der Spiegel and Le Monde) published reports
  based on more than 15 million leaked internal documents.
- The files show the bank kept Russian oligarch clients — including Oleg
  Deripaska and Viktor Vekselberg — despite years of internal compliance
  warnings, and handled far more suspicious transactions linked to Jeffrey
  Epstein than previously known (about $580 million, per Le Monde). The bank
  also mistakenly shared Donald Trump's tax records with another client's
  accountant. Deutsche Bank called the issues historical and said it had
  spent €7 billion on controls since 2019; shares closed down 6% on Tuesday,
  their biggest one-day fall in more than a year.
- Sources: Reuters, 17 Nov 2026 18:54 UTC
  (https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/analysis-deutsche-bank-leaks-test-ceo-sewings-pledge-reform/
  — Reuters by John O'Donnell and Tom Sims, also carried by other outlets);
  Financial Times reporting via SourceFirst News
  (https://sourcefirstnews.com/deutsche-bank-leak-15-million-files-shares-russia-epstein-trump).
  Date note: first published 17 Nov 18:54 UTC, i.e. 18 Nov 01:54 WIB; the
  reports continued to be published through 18 Nov.

### 6. RUSSIA — Military transport plane crashes in Siberia; toll disputed
- A Russian Antonov An-12 military transport aircraft crashed in an
  unpopulated area near the village of Yagunovo, Kemerovo region, in
  south-western Siberia, on Tuesday, killing everyone on board. The Russian
  Defence Ministry said the preliminary cause was a technical malfunction; a
  commission from the Russian Aerospace Forces is investigating.
- The death toll is disputed: the Defence Ministry, quoted by TASS/RIA, said
  19 people were on board and all perished; Izvestia, cited by Reuters,
  reported 17 killed. The image states no toll number.
- Sources: CNN

  Al Jazeera (via etleboro.org and dailyprabhat.com).

### 7. UKRAINE / WAR — UN: 2026 already Ukraine's deadliest year for civilians
- The UN Human Rights Monitoring Mission in Ukraine (HRMMU) said attacks in
  October 2026 left at least 372 civilians dead and 1,981 injured — 95% more
  than in October 2025, and the highest October toll since the full-scale
  invasion began. Long-range weapons (missiles and drones) caused 55% of the
  casualties.
- Civilian deaths in the first ten months of 2026 have already exceeded the
  total for all of 2025, which in turn was worse than 2024. In all, almost
  18,000 civilians have been killed and almost 58,000 injured since February
  2022.
- Sources: UN News, published 2026-11-17T16:16:21-05:00
  (https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168828);
  HRMMU/OHCHR press release, Kyiv, 17 Nov 2026
  (https://ukraine.ohchr.org/en/In-Ukraine-Civilians-Die-at-Alarming-Rates-Amid-Intensifying-Attacks-UN-Human-Rights-Officials-Say).
  Date note: announced 17 Nov, i.e. 18 Nov 04:16 WIB.
