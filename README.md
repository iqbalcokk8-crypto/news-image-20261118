## About the image

`entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) is a newspaper-style front page. The lead story is
the Gulf flaring up again overnight — Bahrain's all-clear after fresh alerts,
Kuwait intercepting drones, and US jets striking a missile site on Iran's
Qeshm Island; the six other stories cover APEC opening in Shenzhen, Nvidia's
record quarter and its board refusing to reinstate Jensen Huang, the 27-year
sentence for the Iran-backed plot to kill Trump, the 15-million-file Deutsche
Bank leak, a Russian military plane crashing in Siberia, and the UN's report
that 2026 is already Ukraine's deadliest year for civilians. Every fact on
the image was checked against at least two independent reports — see
`RESEARCH.md` for the sources and verification notes. The image is drawn by
`gen_image.py` (Python PIL, no AI image generator, no copyrighted images);
`main.ts` copies the finished PNG to the output directory and verifies it.
